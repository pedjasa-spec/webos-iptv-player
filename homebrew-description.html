This is a fork of lennylxx/webos-iptv-player. It adds the following on top of the original:

  • Xtream category filter: load only the live categories you choose, which keeps large provider lists fast and within the TV's memory.
  • Channel folders: your own folders for channels. A channel stays in its original group and also appears in its folder.
  • Starred groups and categories: star a group or category to keep it at the top in Live, Movies, and Series.
  • Category sidebar in Movies and Series, like the Live channel list.
  • Settings backup and restore: export and import settings over the local network by scanning a QR code with your phone.
  • Long-press OK menu for Magic Remotes without color buttons.
  • Automatic retry when a live channel fails to start.
  • Channel up/down stays in the group or folder the channel was opened from.

Source: pedjasa-spec/webos-iptv-player
